Privacy Policy — Lockademy: Castilian Spanish

Effective: 4 September 2026

Summary

Lockademy: Castilian Spanish works 100% offline. The developer does not collect, store or share any personal data. The app needs no account and no sign-up. All of your progress is saved on your device only.

Data collected by the developer

None. The app has no server, uses no analytics, and never sends your progress, your favourites or your settings anywhere.

Advertising (sole third party: Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob, the only third-party SDK it integrates. Under Google's own policies, AdMob may collect app interactions, diagnostic data and device identifiers (the advertising ID) for advertising purposes. Read how Google handles that data at policies.google.com. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.

Where it is required (the EEA, for example), the app shows a Google consent form (UMP) before it loads any ad at all. The one-time Remove ads purchase removes all advertising and, with it, any use of the AdMob SDK.

Revoking ad consent

You can change or revoke your consent at any time: open the app and tap the “Ad privacy options” button on the main screen. The Google consent form will appear again.

Permissions the app uses

  • Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): used only as the Android exemption that lets the study card open when you turn the screen on. The app never draws an overlay window, never covers other apps, and never bypasses your device's security lock (your PIN, pattern or fingerprint keeps working exactly as before).
  • Notifications: local study reminders and the persistent notification for the lock-screen service. All local, all offline.
  • Ignore battery optimizations (optional): keeps the service that detects screen-on from being stopped.

Purchases

The Remove ads purchase is handled by Google Play Billing. The developer neither receives nor keeps any payment data.

Children

The app is a vocabulary tool suitable for people aged 13 and over; it is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this same page.
